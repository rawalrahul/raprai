## RAPR AI 2.0.4

### Connect any app with an MCP server
Click **+ Add MCP server** in RAPR Packages and paste the server's URL, its command, or the JSON snippet from its docs (the one written for Claude Desktop, Cursor or VS Code). Sign in by account, device code (for servers without a browser), API key or headers. Every AI in RAPR can then use its tools: Claude, Codex and Gemini directly, and Ollama and API AIs through RAPR.

### Memory that keeps up with you
When a session ends, an AI now reads what you typed, next to the memories that look related, and decides what to do: save a new lasting fact, update one that changed ("we moved from SQLite to Postgres"), or archive one that's no longer true. One-off requests like "fix this bug" are skipped. Before, a few fixed phrases were matched and nothing was ever corrected.
- It uses Ollama if it's running on your computer; otherwise, for a Claude session, Claude's small Haiku model. A conversation is never sent somewhere it didn't already go. With neither, RAPR matches phrases like before.
- Only your own messages are read, never the AI's replies, so a web page an AI looked at can't plant memories. Pinned memories are never changed.
- Set `MEMORY_EXTRACTOR` in `.env` to `auto` (default), `ollama`, `claude`, `regex` or `off`.

### An audit log you can trust
Every entry in the approvals log is now signed and linked to the one before it. **Settings → Approvals & audit → Verify** tells you if anything was edited, removed, reordered or added outside RAPR, and on which line.
Once a day, if the log grew, RAPR also keeps a checkpoint (how many entries, and the latest signature). It sends the checkpoint to every chat app you've linked: Telegram, WhatsApp, and Discord or Slack once you've messaged RAPR there. Without any linked, it's kept on your computer. Verify checks against it, so entries cut from the end are caught too. `AUDIT_ANCHOR=0` stops the sending.

### A new look
- **Sidebar:** an icon rail on the left; the section you pick fills the panel. Drag icons to reorder them, or click the open one to tuck the panel away. Prefer the old list? Choose the **Stacked** layout.
- **Customize:** theme, accent colour, font, interface size, corners, density, reduced motion, chat width and text size. Open it from the rail, from Settings, or by right-clicking a rail icon.
- **Message box:** one rounded box with your message on top and a slim toolbar underneath: add files, the AI picker, a voice-message mic, and one button that is Send, Stop or hands-free voice depending on what you're doing.
- **Choose project:** recent projects, Home, Desktop, Documents, Downloads and drives on the side; click any part of the path, or type one; filter as you type; make a **New folder**. Hidden and system folders are tucked away unless you ask for them.

### Group chats, everywhere and livelier
- A group chat shows up in the app and in every linked chat app, wherever it started. Replies show up live in the app, even for a group used from WhatsApp.
- **Keep chat apps in sync** (Settings, on by default): your messages and the AI's replies in every session reach every linked chat app. Turn it off and each app only gets replies to what was sent from it.
- AI replies in group chats are formatted (bold, code, lists) instead of showing raw Markdown.
- **Kelvin's stage:** each AI in the group is a Kelvin in its own colours, acting out its part. The one replying works, the one that just answered celebrates, and the baton flies to whoever's turn it is. Kelvin also acts out more scenes on his own, on the desktop too, and shows "working" while an AI runs commands.

### Updates you can see
RAPR checks every hour whether there's something new, puts a dot on Settings, and **Settings → Updates** lists what changed with an **Update now** button (or run `rapr update`). Open tabs pick up the new version on their own; the tab you're looking at asks first, so nothing you were typing is lost. Turn the check off in the same place, or with `UPDATE_CHECK=0`.

### Fixes
- **Two projects at once:** choosing another project while the AI is still working starts a parallel session there, instead of moving the busy one and making you wait.
- **Resume** from History starts its own session with the AI and folder that conversation used, instead of joining whichever session was open.
- **Windows:** `rapr update` no longer fails with "being used by another process", and no longer stops itself partway.
- **Groq, OpenRouter, GitHub Models and local servers:** fixed "No module named 'helm'" in sessions, group chats, AI Council and bake-off, and emoji or non-English replies on Windows.
- **Codex:** only lists the models your sign-in can use, and switches to its default model when one is refused. Codex's config file stays valid when RAPR adds MCP servers.
- New sessions start in your home folder, not RAPR's app folder.
- Skills are matched to what you typed; even "Hi" used to pick the file-organizer skill.
- Generated workflows no longer stop to wait for you at steps that don't ask you anything.
