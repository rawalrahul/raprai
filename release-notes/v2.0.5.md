## RAPR AI 2.0.5

### Use RAPR inside Claude Code and Codex
RAPR is now an MCP server, so the AI you're coding with can ask the others for help. Add it once:
```
claude mcp add --scope user rapr -- rapr mcp     # Claude Code
codex mcp add rapr -- rapr mcp                   # Codex
```
On Windows, end either with `-- cmd /c rapr mcp`. For Codex, also add `tool_timeout_sec = 600` under `[mcp_servers.rapr]` in `~/.codex/config.toml`: Codex gives up on a tool after 60 seconds, and a review usually takes a few minutes. Cursor, Windsurf and other clients: add a server with the command `rapr` and the argument `mcp`.

Then ask in plain words, like *"get a second opinion from Codex on my changes"*:
- **second_opinion:** another AI reviews your uncommitted changes (or the last commit, or changes since a branch) and reports bugs, most serious first.
- **ask_agent:** put any question or task to another AI.
- **start_bakeoff:** 2 to 4 AIs try the same task, each in its own copy of the project; merge the one you like. `bakeoff_status` answers within 50 seconds, so it fits inside Codex's limit even without the setting above.
- **list_agents:** the AIs installed and signed in on this computer.

The other AI works in a temporary copy of your project, so your files only change if you allow edits or merge a bake-off. It runs on your computer with your own sign-ins; nothing is hosted and the RAPR app doesn't need to be open. See the [guide](../docs/guide.md#use-rapr-from-claude-code-cursor-or-codex-mcp).

### Share a bake-off, and RAPR Arena
After you merge a winner (or discard them all), **🏆 Share result** opens a card to copy, download or post on X. **Publish to RAPR Arena** adds it to the public leaderboard at [raprai.com/arena](https://www.raprai.com/arena), which shows which AI wins which kind of task.
- Publishing is your choice, and **Exactly what gets sent** shows the request first: the AIs and models, times, files and lines changed, test results and which one you merged.
- Never sent: your code, file names, folder paths or what the AIs said. The task text only if you tick the box.

### Your month with RAPR
A shareable card of your month: tasks sent, active days and longest streak, your AI crew and its MVP, bake-offs judged, and a title for the month. Open it from the top of the **Usage** panel or the AI menu; RAPR offers last month's once at the start of each month. It's worked out on your computer and nothing is uploaded.

### Hey Kelvin
Start a message (typed or spoken) with `@kelvin`, **Hey Kelvin** or just **Kelvin,** and the session you have open answers it. With nothing open, or when that AI is busy or over budget, RAPR picks one: the cheapest set-up AI for a greeting or short question, the strongest for coding. Name an AI right after ("Hey Kelvin, `@codex` …", "ask Gemini to …") to choose it yourself.

### Voice
- **Voice messages on WhatsApp, Discord and Slack**, as on Telegram: they're transcribed on your computer and handled like typed text. Slack needs the `files:read` scope.
- **Hands-free sounds natural and starts sooner.** Replies are read a sentence at a time in Kokoro, a free voice that runs on your computer (a one-time ~120 MB download). Talk over a reply to interrupt it.
- **Local, private:** a new mode that hears you with Whisper on your computer, which also works in browsers without speech recognition. Speech-to-text is about 4 times faster.
- **Live:** with an OpenAI or Gemini API key, talk with Kelvin in real time; he hands real work to your sessions and reads you the result. The browser only gets a short-lived token, never your key.
- Pick the mode in **Settings → Voices → Hands-free voice**.

### Fixes
- Voice messages no longer fail with "unexpected keyword argument 'metadata_errors'".
- "Kelvin, what's the day today?" (voice often drops the "Hey") is understood instead of answering "No session is focused".
- A bake-off discarded while it was running is no longer marked done when its AIs finish.
- The Windows `.exe` installer is retired. Windows installs with the PowerShell command in the [README](../README.md#install), which keeps your chats and settings.
