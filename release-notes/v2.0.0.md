## RAPR AI 2.0.0

### Your AIs, working together
**Group chats.** Put several AIs in one conversation. Pick the AIs and RAPR starts them for you. Ask once and they all answer, reading and building on each other's replies. `@mention` a member (or `@all`) to choose who answers. Works from the app, Telegram (`/group`) and WhatsApp.

**Crews.** Ready-made group chats where every AI has a job: Code review, Research, Launch, and Debate to decision.

**Bake-off.** Give the same coding task to several AIs. Each one works in its own copy of your project. Compare the results side by side (files changed, diff, whether your tests passed, time taken), then merge the one you like.

**Smart routing.** Choose "Auto" and RAPR picks the AI for each task: free local models for easy tasks, the strongest coding AI for hard ones, skipping any AI that is over budget. It tells you why it chose.

**More AIs.** Add any agent that speaks the Agent Client Protocol (ACP) in Settings → Agents over ACP, and use it like any other AI.

### Control it from anywhere
**WhatsApp control.** Link RAPR to your WhatsApp by scanning a QR code (free, no Business API). Talk to your sessions and group chats from your "Message yourself" chat.

**Discord and Slack.** Add your bot's token to RAPR's `.env` file and talk to RAPR from Discord or Slack too.

**Phone alerts.** Install RAPR on your phone as a web app and get a notification when an action needs your approval, even when the app is closed. Free, no account needed.

**Run it on your own server.** Settings → Cloud server: enter your server's address and login and RAPR installs itself there with Docker, with a free public address. Works with Oracle's free server and any Linux server. Kelvin keeps working on Telegram and WhatsApp. You can still use RAPR on your laptop as before.

### Safer and easier
**Your own approval rules.** Decide what always needs a yes, what is never allowed, and what can go ahead without asking. Every approval and decision is recorded in an audit log on your computer.

**Playbook library.** See the how-to notes RAPR has learned from past tasks, and switch any of them on or off.

**Voices.** Each AI in a group chat can read its replies aloud in its own voice, and hands-free mode lets you talk instead of type.

**Kelvin, on your desktop.** Resize Kelvin with the mouse wheel (small, medium, large), hide him with the ✕ or "Hide for 1 hour", or show him only while an AI is working. Kelvin also shows a crew while a group chat answers.

**Cleaner header.** One sidebar button per side, labelled Group chat and Council buttons, and icons that stay consistent in light and dark themes.

**Fixes.** Security and stability updates from the previous release.
