## RAPR AI 2.1.0

### Your brief
Every morning, one short list of what needs you, from **all** the AIs you use. **Settings → Your brief**.
- **Inside RAPR**, at no cost: approvals waiting for you, sessions you left unfinished, and what your scheduled tasks answered (failures first).
- **Through each AI's own apps.** Claude Code looks through your claude.ai connectors (Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Drive…) and its MCP servers; Codex and Gemini through theirs. RAPR doesn't copy any connection or sign-in: it asks the AI that has the app. Add several AIs, or the same AI twice, to cover several accounts (a work Gmail through Claude, a personal one through Codex).
- **One tap to hand it off.** Each item can come with a next step. **▶ Hand to Claude** (or whichever AI found it) starts a session with the item and its link as the task.
- Arrives on Telegram (with buttons), WhatsApp, Discord and Slack (`/brief`, `/brief now`, `/do 2`, `/skip 2`), as a phone notification, and in the app.
- Optional check-ins every 2, 3 or 4 hours, only when something new needs you.
- The looks only read: anything an AI tries to send or change during one is refused and listed as a suggestion instead. They run on your own AI plans like any task, and don't feed RAPR's learning or skill guides. While the brief is on, the old Heartbeat check-in stays quiet.

### Needs you
A new first section in the sidebar collects everything waiting for you: approvals (approve or deny right there), your brief's items, and scheduled task results. The bell shows how many.

### What your AIs can reach
**Settings → Your brief → What your AIs can reach** lists the apps each AI is already connected to (from `claude mcp list`, `codex mcp list` and `gemini mcp list`) and RAPR's own, and suggests brief lines from them.

### Asks before acting in your apps
When an AI wants to **send an email, post a message, comment, merge, pay, sign or delete** something in a connected app, it now asks you first, like a risky command: in the app, on Telegram, WhatsApp and your phone, with the details (recipient, subject, channel, pull request…). Reading never asks.
- Covers everything Claude Code reaches (RAPR's MCP servers, the ones you added to Claude Code, and claude.ai connectors) through a hook RAPR adds when it starts Claude, and tools any AI calls through RAPR itself. Codex and Gemini call the apps in their own settings directly (RAPR's included, which it copies there), so those follow Codex's and Gemini's own approval settings.
- **Your rules now apply to every tool call**: `allow` + `slack.*post` never asks for Slack posts, `deny` + `merge` never lets an AI merge, `ask` + `postgres.*query` asks even for a read. Every decision is in the audit log.
- With no answer within 10 minutes, the action is skipped and the AI is told it's waiting for you. Change the wait, or turn asking off, in **Settings → Approvals & audit**.

### In your editor
RAPR's MCP server has a new `latest_brief` tool: ask Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Windsurf *"what needs me today?"*.
